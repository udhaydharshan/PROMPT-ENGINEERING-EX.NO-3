3. Preceding Question Prompting:
   a)
   Climate change is a global concern because it affects the entire planet, leading to rising temperatures, extreme weather events, sea-level rise, loss of biodiversity, and serious impacts on human health, food security, and economies worldwide. These effects do not stop at national borders, making climate change a shared challenge that requires international cooperation.

Greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide, methane, and nitrous oxide contribute to global warming by trapping heat in the Earth’s atmosphere. Sunlight enters the atmosphere and warms the Earth’s surface, which then releases heat back toward space. Greenhouse gases absorb and re-radiate this heat, preventing it from escaping completely. As human activities increase the concentration of these gases, more heat is trapped, causing the Earth’s average temperature to rise.

   b)
   Vaccines work by training the immune system to recognize and fight harmful germs without causing the actual disease. A vaccine contains a weakened, inactivated, or harmless piece of a virus or bacterium. When it is introduced into the body, the immune system treats it as a threat and produces antibodies to destroy it.

This process, called immunization, also helps the body remember the germ. If the person is exposed to the real virus or bacteria later, the immune system recognizes it quickly and fights it off before it can cause serious illness.
